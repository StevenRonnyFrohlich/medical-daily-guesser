    Six fields. Four calls each.

    Each calendar day you get six microscope images. Each one has four diagnoses. Guess once per field, then copy a Wordle-style tray.

    • The six boxes track the day: green is a hit, red is a miss.
    • Stain and magnification are on the requisition. Use them.
    • Wrong answers are lookalikes, not random noise.
    • Finish all six to keep a streak. Stored in this browser.
    • Open lab is another six-field tray and does not touch your streak.
    • Keys 1–4 or A–D select an answer. Click the field to enlarge it.

    Images are public-domain or freely licensed teaching files, mostly CDC and Wikimedia Commons. This is a game, not medical advice.
