Biomedical engineer. I worked at a startup that started out trying to improve life in the third world. We had to pivot. Most people will not invest in problems that mainly affect the poor.

Years on the scope: histology (gross morphology and cancer resections), cytology (liquid-based cytology and Pap smears), and hematology — including rare blood disorders you can still call from a smear.

I built The Call as a practice scope: look at the field, make the call, learn the differences that usually take a specialist. The tools exist now to make that work more engaging, strip out most of the normal work, and leave them the truly difficult diagnoses — the ones that save lives.

I still want that help where it is scarcest. Start with the people at the bottom.
